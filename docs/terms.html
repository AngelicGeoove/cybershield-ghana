Terms of Use

Purpose

CyberShield Ghana is a reporting channel for suspected cybercrime in Ghana, operated in support of the Cyber Security Authority. It is not a substitute for an emergency service.

Accurate information

You agree to provide truthful information. Deliberately filing a false report may constitute an offence under Ghanaian law.

How reports are handled

Submitting a report does not guarantee a specific outcome. Cases are prioritised based on risk and available resources. You will be able to track the status of your report and receive messages from investigators.

Prohibited use

You may not use this service to harass, threaten, or submit abusive content.

Service availability

The service requires internet access and may be unavailable during maintenance. Keep your report reference safe, as it is needed to track your case.

Contact

Questions: report@csa.gov.gh
