Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you file a report we collect the details you provide: your name, contact information, the description of the incident, and any additional information you choose to share. We do not ask for or store more personal data than is needed to investigate your case.

How it is used

Your information is used by the Cyber Security Authority and authorised investigators to respond to your report. It is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

Reports are stored in Google Cloud (Firebase) infrastructure used by the Cyber Security Authority. Evidence files provided through the desktop application remain on the device that submitted them and are only shared when required for an investigation.

Your rights

You may view, export or delete your own reports and account data at any time from your profile and reports pages. Deletion requests are honoured within 30 days.

Contact

Questions about this policy: report@csa.gov.gh
